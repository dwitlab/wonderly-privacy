Privacy Policy – Wonderly

Effective date: September 30, 2026
App: Wonderly – AR Preschool Learning
Developer: Dwit Labs

Wonderly is an augmented reality (AR) learning application designed to help preschool children explore letters, numbers, shapes, colors, and educational objects through interactive experiences.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Wonderly. We aim to protect children’s privacy and to describe the role of third-party services used by the app.

2. Information We Collect

Wonderly does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, or phone number to use its core learning features. Dwit Labs does not operate a user registration system in the current version of Wonderly.

Wonderly uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process certain information to provide advertising services, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and maintain service functionality. Depending on SDK behavior and configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address;
  • device and account identifiers, where permitted;
  • app interactions, such as ad impressions and interactions; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be processed by Google and applicable advertising service providers according to their privacy policies and applicable child-directed advertising requirements.

3. Camera and Augmented Reality

Wonderly uses the device camera locally to support AR functionality, including surface detection and placement of virtual classroom objects.

Dwit Labs does not record, store, or transmit camera images or video to its own servers. Camera access is used for AR functionality and is not used by Dwit Labs for advertising or analytics. Users can disable camera permission through their device settings, although AR functionality may no longer work.

4. Advertising

Wonderly displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Because Wonderly is designed for preschool children, we configure Google Mobile Ads to apply child-directed advertising treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G.

We do not intentionally serve personalized advertising or behavioral remarketing to children. Google AdMob and applicable advertising service providers may process certain device information, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information as described in their applicable privacy policies. Advertising is subject to Google’s applicable policies and children’s privacy requirements.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · Google AdMob Help

5. Children’s Privacy

Wonderly is designed for preschool children and their parents. We do not intentionally request children’s names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location information. Wonderly does not require user accounts to access its core educational features.

The application uses Google AdMob for advertising, configured for child-directed treatment. Certain information may be processed automatically by the advertising SDK as described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not knowingly use Wonderly to collect personal information directly from children for account creation or behavioral advertising. Parents or legal guardians may contact Dwit Labs with questions or privacy-related requests concerning the application.

6. Third-Party Services

Wonderly currently uses the following third-party services and technologies:

  • Google AdMob – advertising services. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services – Android platform functionality. Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity and AR Foundation – application development and augmented reality functionality. Unity Privacy Policy

These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and applicable terms. Dwit Labs does not currently use a separate analytics service such as Firebase Analytics in Wonderly.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by Wonderly. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their respective security practices, including applicable encryption and security measures. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Wonderly does not operate a user account system in its current version. Dwit Labs does not maintain a user account database for the core learning experience.

Information processed by Google AdMob and other third-party services is subject to the respective providers’ retention and deletion practices. Parents or legal guardians may contact Dwit Labs at dwitlabs@gmail.com with privacy-related questions or requests concerning information handled by Dwit Labs. Requests concerning information processed directly by third-party providers may also need to be directed to those providers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date shown at the top of this page. We encourage parents and guardians to review this page periodically.
